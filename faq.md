# Frequently Asked Questions

## Locations & Search

**Why is my location not showing?**

Try searching using a nearby city name or check the spelling. Smaller surf spots are sometimes listed under the nearest coastal town. You can also search by region or coordinates.

**Can I save multiple locations?**

Yes. Click ⭐ on any location to add it to your favorites. Saved locations appear under the search bar, and you can reorder or rename them in **Settings → Saved Locations**.

## Data & Forecasts

**Why are weather values delayed?**

Data refreshes every few minutes depending on provider updates. A timestamp on the dashboard's Current Conditions panel shows when the last update was received.

**How far ahead can I see forecasts?**

The hourly forecast covers the next 24 hours, and the weekly forecast covers seven days.

**Can I change the units (Celsius/Fahrenheit)?**

Yes. Go to **Settings → Units** to switch temperature, wave height, and wind speed units. Changes apply instantly across the app.

## Account & Login

**I cannot log in**

- Verify your email and password
- Reset your password using "Forgot Password" on the login page
- Check your internet connection

**How do I update my profile details?**

Open the profile menu and go to **Settings → Profile** to change your display name, photo, email address, or password.

## Alerts

**Why am I not receiving alerts?**

Check that alerts are enabled in **Settings → Alerts** and that browser/app notification permissions are allowed. See the [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) guide for detailed steps.
