# Quick Start

Welcome to Surf Weather.

This guide helps you start using the platform in under 5 minutes. By the end, you will be signed in, viewing live surf conditions for your favorite spot, and have it saved for quick access.

## Step 1 — Sign In

Log in using your registered email and password.

1. Open the Surf Weather app or website.
2. Enter your registered email address and password.
3. Click **Sign In**.

> New here? Select **Create Account** on the login page, confirm your email address, and then sign in.

## Step 2 — Select Location

Use the search bar to find your coastal city or beach.

1. Click the search bar at the top of the dashboard.
2. Type a spot name, city, or region — for example, `Goa` or `Bondi Beach`.
3. Pick the correct result from the suggestions.

The dashboard reloads immediately with data for the selected location.

## Step 3 — View Surf Conditions

The dashboard displays:

- **Wave height** — current significant wave height
- **Wind speed** — including direction, so you can judge onshore vs. offshore conditions
- **Temperature** — air and water temperature
- **Tide levels** — current tide state and the next tide change

Scroll down for the hourly forecast (next 24 hours) and the weekly outlook. See the [User Manual](user-manual.md) for a full tour of the dashboard.

## Step 4 — Save Favorite Locations

Click ⭐ to save locations for quick access later.

- Saved locations appear under the search bar for one-tap switching.
- Your top favorite becomes the default dashboard view when you sign in.
- Favorites sync across devices linked to your account.
