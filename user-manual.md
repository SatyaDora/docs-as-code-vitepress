# User Manual

This manual explains all major features available to users. It walks you through the dashboard, location search, alerts, and account settings, with step-by-step instructions and tips for getting the most out of the app.

## Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is the first screen you see after signing in. It provides real-time surf and weather insights for your currently selected location, combining live observations with forecast data so you can plan your session at a glance.

### Key Sections

#### Current Conditions

The Current Conditions panel shows what is happening right now at your selected location:

- **Wave height** — the current significant wave height, displayed in feet or meters depending on your unit settings
- **Swell direction and period** — where the swell is coming from and the seconds between waves; longer periods generally mean more powerful, better-organized surf
- **Wind speed and direction** — onshore winds typically make conditions choppy, while light offshore winds groom the waves
- **Air and water temperature** — so you can pick the right wetsuit or rash guard
- **Tide state** — whether the tide is rising, falling, or at high/low slack, along with the time of the next tide change

Data in this panel refreshes automatically every few minutes. A timestamp at the bottom of the panel shows when the last update was received.

#### Hourly Forecast

The Hourly Forecast panel shows expected conditions hour by hour for the next 24 hours. Scroll horizontally to move through the timeline. Each hour displays wave height, wind, and a condition rating so you can quickly spot the best window — for example, a morning glass-off before the wind picks up at midday.

Tap any hour to expand it and see full detail, including swell period, tide height, and precipitation probability.

#### Weekly Forecast

The Weekly Forecast panel extends the outlook to seven days. Each day is summarized with:

- Expected wave height range (minimum to maximum)
- Dominant swell direction
- Wind trend for the day
- An overall condition rating (Poor, Fair, Good, or Epic)

Use this view to plan ahead — for instance, spotting a swell arriving midweek so you can arrange time off in advance.

#### Alerts Panel

The Alerts Panel sits at the top of the dashboard whenever an active alert applies to your location. It shows all triggered alerts — such as high waves or storm warnings — with their severity level and expiry time. See the **Alerts** section below for setup details.

## Location Search

Use the search bar at the top of the dashboard to switch between locations.

To change your location:

1. Click or tap the search bar.
2. Type the name of a beach, surf spot, or coastal town — suggestions appear as you type.
3. Select a result from the list. The dashboard immediately reloads with data for the new location.

Tips:

- You can search by spot name ("Pipeline"), region ("North Shore Oahu"), or coordinates.
- Recently viewed locations appear below the search bar for one-tap access.
- Save frequently used spots to your favorites (see **Account Settings**) so they sync across your devices.

## Alerts

Alerts notify you when conditions at your saved locations meet thresholds you care about, so you never miss a good session — or get caught out by dangerous weather.

Users can enable alerts for:

- **High waves** — triggered when wave height exceeds a limit you set (for example, notify me when waves go above 6 ft). Useful both for chasing big swells and for safety warnings.
- **Strong winds** — triggered when sustained wind speed passes your chosen threshold, warning you of blown-out conditions or hazardous offshore winds.
- **Storm warnings** — triggered automatically when official meteorological services issue a storm, cyclone, or severe weather advisory for one of your saved locations. These cannot be disabled, as they are safety-critical.

To configure alerts:

1. Open the profile menu and select **Alerts**.
2. Choose the saved location the alert applies to.
3. Toggle on the alert types you want.
4. For high waves and strong winds, set your preferred threshold values.
5. Choose how you want to be notified — push notification, email, or both.

Active and recently expired alerts remain visible in the dashboard's Alerts Panel until they are dismissed.

## Account Settings

From the profile menu in the top-right corner, you can manage everything related to your account.

### Change Units (Celsius/Fahrenheit)

Switch between metric and imperial units at any time:

1. Open the profile menu and select **Settings**.
2. Under **Units**, choose your preference for temperature (Celsius/Fahrenheit), wave height (feet/meters), and wind speed (knots, km/h, or mph).
3. Changes apply instantly across the dashboard, forecasts, and alerts.

### Manage Saved Locations

Your saved locations (favorites) are listed under **Settings > Saved Locations**. From here you can:

- Add a new location using the search bar
- Reorder the list by dragging — the top location becomes your default dashboard view
- Remove locations you no longer need
- Rename locations with custom labels (for example, "Home break" or "Holiday spot")

### Update Profile Details

Under **Settings > Profile** you can:

- Change your display name and profile photo
- Update the email address associated with your account
- Change your password
- Manage notification preferences and connected devices

Remember to save your changes before leaving the page. If you update your email address, you will receive a confirmation link at the new address — your settings take effect once you confirm.
