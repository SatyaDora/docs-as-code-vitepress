# Troubleshooting

Use this guide to resolve the most common issues. Each problem lists likely causes and step-by-step solutions.

---

## Problem: Dashboard not loading

**Possible causes**

- Slow internet connection
- Temporary server delay
- Corrupted browser cache

**Solution**

1. Refresh the page.
2. Clear browser cache.
3. Try again after a few minutes.
4. If the problem persists, try a different browser or device to isolate the issue.

---

## Problem: No weather data displayed

**Possible causes**

- Invalid location
- Data provider delay

**Solution**

1. Select another nearby location.
2. Check again after refreshing.
3. Confirm the location name against a nearby coastal city if the spot is small or newly added.

---

## Problem: Alerts not working

**Possible causes**

- Alerts disabled in settings
- Notification permissions blocked

**Solution**

1. Go to Settings → Alerts.
2. Enable notifications.
3. Allow browser notifications.
4. Verify the alert thresholds — alerts only trigger when conditions exceed the values you set.

---

## Problem: Cannot log in

**Possible causes**

- Incorrect email or password
- Unconfirmed account

**Solution**

1. Re-enter your credentials carefully (watch for caps lock).
2. Use **Forgot Password** to reset your password.
3. Check your inbox (and spam folder) for the account confirmation email.

---

## Problem: Units or settings not saving

**Possible causes**

- Changes not saved before leaving the page
- Browser blocking cookies or local storage

**Solution**

1. Click **Save** before navigating away from Settings.
2. Allow cookies and local storage for the Surf Weather site.
3. Sign out and back in to force a settings sync.

---

To help us resolve your issue faster, please include:

- The email address on your account
- The location(s) affected
- A screenshot of the problem, if possible
- The browser and device you are using
